## Overview

IBM and Red Hat expanded Project Lightwell on July 8 with two new commercial offerings, Lightwell Network and Lightwell Clearinghouse Premier, designed to help enterprises patch and consume open source software at scale, according to [IBM Newsroom](https://newsroom.ibm.com/2026-07-08-ibm-and-red-hat-expand-lightwell-with-new-commercial-offerings-to-build-the-trust-infrastructure-for-ai-era-open-source). The launch builds on a $5 billion commitment to open source security that IBM and Red Hat first announced in May 2026, according to [IBM Newsroom](https://newsroom.ibm.com/2026-07-08-ibm-and-red-hat-expand-lightwell-with-new-commercial-offerings-to-build-the-trust-infrastructure-for-ai-era-open-source).

## What We Know

- Lightwell Network is now generally available, according to [IBM Newsroom](https://newsroom.ibm.com/2026-07-08-ibm-and-red-hat-expand-lightwell-with-new-commercial-offerings-to-build-the-trust-infrastructure-for-ai-era-open-source), which describes it as "providing immediate access to an active and growing library of content spanning latest to legacy libraries with high-value remediations." The network's initial catalog spans more than 6,500 remediated, digitally signed and certified application-layer dependencies across ecosystems including Java and Python, according to [IBM Newsroom](https://newsroom.ibm.com/2026-07-08-ibm-and-red-hat-expand-lightwell-with-new-commercial-offerings-to-build-the-trust-infrastructure-for-ai-era-open-source).
- Lightwell Clearinghouse Premier is entering a "limited-availability commercial onboarding phase" designed to serve as "a trusted intermediary for deep industry collaboration, advanced vertical threat coordination, and secured patch embargoes," according to [IBM Newsroom](https://newsroom.ibm.com/2026-07-08-ibm-and-red-hat-expand-lightwell-with-new-commercial-offerings-to-build-the-trust-infrastructure-for-ai-era-open-source). Its initial launch is limited to the financial services industry, with IBM and Red Hat planning to expand it to "government, healthcare, and telecommunications" in future phases, according to [IBM Newsroom](https://newsroom.ibm.com/2026-07-08-ibm-and-red-hat-expand-lightwell-with-new-commercial-offerings-to-build-the-trust-infrastructure-for-ai-era-open-source).
- IBM frames the need for the offerings with a set of figures on open source's footprint and risk: open source is "comprising up to 90% of enterprise codebases and driving 9.8 trillion downloads in 2025 alone," while "$50 AI-generated exploits have broken traditional patch management, leaving codebases with an average of 581 vulnerabilities," according to [IBM Newsroom](https://newsroom.ibm.com/2026-07-08-ibm-and-red-hat-expand-lightwell-with-new-commercial-offerings-to-build-the-trust-infrastructure-for-ai-era-open-source).
- "Lightwell represents a fundamental structural shift in how we secure all enterprise software," said Matt Hicks, President and CEO of Red Hat. "By pairing automated remediation with our deep engineering heritage, we aim to deliver the trusted infrastructure required to consume open source reliably, sustainably, and at AI speeds," according to [IBM Newsroom](https://newsroom.ibm.com/2026-07-08-ibm-and-red-hat-expand-lightwell-with-new-commercial-offerings-to-build-the-trust-infrastructure-for-ai-era-open-source).
- "IBM and Red Hat are giving enterprises certified fixes they can pull straight into the systems they already run, with no retooling or disruption, backed by a growing network of technology and delivery partners," said Rob Thomas, Senior Vice President, Software & Chief Commercial Officer at IBM, according to [IBM Newsroom](https://newsroom.ibm.com/2026-07-08-ibm-and-red-hat-expand-lightwell-with-new-commercial-offerings-to-build-the-trust-infrastructure-for-ai-era-open-source).
- The commercial launch builds on Project Lightwell, which IBM and its subsidiary Red Hat first announced in May 2026 as "a joint initiative backed by a $5 billion investment and a workforce of more than 20,000 engineers," according to [SecurityWeek](https://www.securityweek.com/ibm-and-red-hat-commit-5-billion-to-secure-open-source-supply-chains-under-project-lightwell/). At the time, IBM Chairman and CEO Arvind Krishna said, "Open source is the backbone of today's digital economy and the foundation of modern AI, and we are at an inflection point in how it is built, secured, and scaled," according to [SecurityWeek](https://www.securityweek.com/ibm-and-red-hat-commit-5-billion-to-secure-open-source-supply-chains-under-project-lightwell/).
- The clearinghouse at the center of Lightwell "will use AI to identify, triage, prioritize, and validate vulnerabilities and fixes across open source code bases," according to [SecurityWeek](https://www.securityweek.com/ibm-and-red-hat-commit-5-billion-to-secure-open-source-supply-chains-under-project-lightwell/). [SDxCentral](https://www.sdxcentral.com/news/ibm-red-hat-commit-5b-to-open-source-security-initiative/) independently described the same clearinghouse as "a security coordination layer that uses AI to validate and test fixes across large volumes of open-source code."
- Initial participants named when Project Lightwell was announced in May included Bank of America, BNY, Citi, Goldman Sachs, JPMorganChase, Mastercard, Morgan Stanley, Royal Bank of Canada, State Street, Visa, and Wells Fargo, according to [SecurityWeek](https://www.securityweek.com/ibm-and-red-hat-commit-5-billion-to-secure-open-source-supply-chains-under-project-lightwell/).
- The stakes extend well beyond that initial group of banks: more than 90% of Fortune 500 companies rely on open-source technologies, according to [SDxCentral](https://www.sdxcentral.com/news/ibm-red-hat-commit-5b-to-open-source-security-initiative/).

## What We Don't Know

- IBM and Red Hat have not disclosed subscription pricing for either Lightwell Network or Lightwell Clearinghouse Premier.
- Neither company has given a timeline for when Clearinghouse Premier's planned expansion into government, healthcare, and telecommunications will happen, beyond describing it as coming "in future phases," according to [IBM Newsroom](https://newsroom.ibm.com/2026-07-08-ibm-and-red-hat-expand-lightwell-with-new-commercial-offerings-to-build-the-trust-infrastructure-for-ai-era-open-source).
- It is not yet clear how many enterprise customers beyond the financial institutions named at the May announcement have signed on to the newly launched commercial tiers.
