## Overview

Nothing's Phone (4b) became available exclusively through the company's physical store in London on July 11, ahead of general shipping that begins July 17, [according to Tech Advisor](https://www.techadvisor.com/article/3185803/nothing-phone-4b-cmf-successor.html). The London-based consumer electronics company unveiled the phone globally on July 7 through a livestream event, [according to TechCabal](https://techcabal.com/2026/07/07/nothing-phone-4b-price-release-date-specs/). It starts at £299 in the UK and €329 in the eurozone for a model with 8GB of RAM and 128GB of storage, [according to Telecompaper](https://www.telecompaper.com/news/nothing-unveils-phone-4b-model--1576471), positioning it as a cheaper entry point below the Phone (4a), which launched at £349, [according to Android Police](https://www.androidpolice.com/nothing-phone-4b-news/).

## What We Know

The Phone (4b) runs on a Qualcomm Snapdragon 6 Gen 4 chipset with 8GB of RAM, [according to Beebom](https://gadgets.beebom.com/mobile/nothing-phone-4b), and is available with either 128GB or 256GB of UFS 2.2 storage, [according to 9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/07/07/nothing-phone-4b-hands-on-launch-price-specs/). Its 6.77-inch AMOLED display runs at a 120Hz refresh rate with a peak brightness of 2,000 nits, [according to TechCabal](https://techcabal.com/2026/07/07/nothing-phone-4b-price-release-date-specs/). The rear camera setup pairs a 50-megapixel main sensor with optical image stabilization and an 8-megapixel ultrawide lens, while the front camera is 16 megapixels, [according to Beebom](https://gadgets.beebom.com/mobile/nothing-phone-4b). The phone carries an IP64 dust and water resistance rating, is 8.6mm thick and weighs 210 grams, [according to Android Police](https://www.androidpolice.com/nothing-phone-4b-news/).

Battery capacity varies by market: international units ship with a 5,200mAh cell, while the India variant carries a 6,000mAh battery, [according to TechCabal](https://techcabal.com/2026/07/07/nothing-phone-4b-price-release-date-specs/). Nothing's own product page describes it as "Our longest-lasting battery," [according to Nothing](https://intl.nothing.tech/products/phone-4b). Charging is rated at 33W wired with 7.5W reverse wired charging, [according to Beebom](https://gadgets.beebom.com/mobile/nothing-phone-4b). On the back, the phone carries a Glyph Bar made up of 45 mini-LEDs spread across four individually addressable zones, [according to Tech Advisor](https://www.techadvisor.com/article/3185803/nothing-phone-4b-cmf-successor.html) — a feature Nothing's own site calls "the focus-saving Glyph Bar," [according to Nothing](https://intl.nothing.tech/products/phone-4b).

The phone ships with Nothing OS 4.1 on top of Android 16, backed by three years of Android OS updates and six years of security patches, [according to TechCabal](https://techcabal.com/2026/07/07/nothing-phone-4b-price-release-date-specs/). In India, the phone goes on sale July 14 through Flipkart and retail partners including Croma, Reliance Digital and Vijay Sales, priced at ₹34,999 for the 8GB/128GB model and ₹38,999 for the 8GB/256GB model, [according to Beebom](https://gadgets.beebom.com/mobile/nothing-phone-4b). Germany and Austria are priced at €329, while Switzerland is priced at CHF 299, [according to TechCabal](https://techcabal.com/2026/07/07/nothing-phone-4b-price-release-date-specs/). The phone comes in black, white and blue color options, [according to Telecompaper](https://www.telecompaper.com/news/nothing-unveils-phone-4b-model--1576471).

## What We Don't Know

Nothing has not announced a release date or pricing for the United States. Android Police reports the phone "has not been announced for release in the US," [according to Android Police](https://www.androidpolice.com/nothing-phone-4b-news/), and Tech Advisor notes that pre-orders are open on Nothing's website even though "it's not going to be available in the US," [according to Tech Advisor](https://www.techadvisor.com/article/3185803/nothing-phone-4b-cmf-successor.html). It is also unclear exactly how the Phone (4b)'s Glyph Bar compares to the one on the Phone (4a): Gizmochina reports the two phones use "a slightly different Glyph Bar layout," [according to Gizmochina](https://www.gizmochina.com/2026/07/08/nothing-phone-4b-vs-phone-4a-which-one-is-better-value/), but Nothing has not detailed the exact differences between the two.

## Analysis

The Phone (4b) sits in an unusually tight gap in Nothing's own lineup: at £299, it costs only £50 less than the £349 Phone (4a), [according to Android Police](https://www.androidpolice.com/nothing-phone-4b-news/). 9to5Google's hands-on review questioned that gap, calling the 4b "a pretty simple step down from Phone (4a)" and writing that it's "impossible to recommend buying Phone (4b) when Phone (4a) is better for basically the same price," [according to 9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/07/07/nothing-phone-4b-hands-on-launch-price-specs/). Gizmochina frames the split more charitably, describing the Phone (4a) as "the more premium mid-ranger, while the 4b focuses on value with a bigger battery," [according to Gizmochina](https://www.gizmochina.com/2026/07/08/nothing-phone-4b-vs-phone-4a-which-one-is-better-value/). The launch also lands as Nothing pursues a broader hardware push under CEO Carl Pei, who has directed the company toward smart glasses and AI-focused earbuds due later this year, as [previously reported](/article/2026-04/03-nothing-plans-ai-smart-glasses-for-2027-and-ai-focused-earbuds-for-later-this-year-as-ceo-carl-pei-embraces-multi-device-strategy).
