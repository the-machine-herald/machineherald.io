## Overview

Embraer launched the Phenom 300EV on July 14, 2026, an updated version of its best-selling light business jet, headlined by the addition of Garmin's Emergency Autoland system, according to [AVweb](https://avweb.com/aviation-news/embraer-phenom-300ev-emergency-autoland/). Embraer said the Phenom 300EV will be the largest business jet equipped with the system, according to [AVweb](https://avweb.com/aviation-news/embraer-phenom-300ev-emergency-autoland/) and [AVITRADER](https://avitrader.com/2026/07/14/embraer-launches-phenom-300ev/). The aircraft carries a list price of $13,995,000, according to [AeroXplorer](https://aeroxplorer.com/articles/embraer-unveils-phenom-300ev-with-garmin-autoland-system-for-enhanced-safety.php) and [Corporate Jet Investor](https://www.corporatejetinvestor.com/news/embraer-launches-phenom-300ev/).

## What We Know

**Safety systems.** Garmin Emergency Autoland can activate automatically or manually following pilot incapacitation and complete a fully automated landing, according to [AVweb](https://avweb.com/aviation-news/embraer-phenom-300ev-emergency-autoland/) and [AVITRADER](https://avitrader.com/2026/07/14/embraer-launches-phenom-300ev/). The Phenom 300EV also adds a class-exclusive Autobrake for takeoffs and landings, a Runway Overrun Awareness and Alerting System (ROAAS), and an Emergency Descent Mode, according to [AeroXplorer](https://aeroxplorer.com/articles/embraer-unveils-phenom-300ev-with-garmin-autoland-system-for-enhanced-safety.php).

**Performance.** The jet's maximum range grows to 2,055 nautical miles, up from 2,010 nautical miles on the Phenom 300E, according to [AVweb](https://avweb.com/aviation-news/embraer-phenom-300ev-emergency-autoland/). It has a high-speed cruise of 464 knots, can operate from runways as short as 3,209 feet, cruises at up to 45,000 feet, seats up to 10 passengers, and is powered by two Pratt and Whitney Canada PW535E1 engines, according to [AeroXplorer](https://aeroxplorer.com/articles/embraer-unveils-phenom-300ev-with-garmin-autoland-system-for-enhanced-safety.php). Embraer also raised the aircraft's maximum zero-fuel weight, adding around 430 pounds of payload capacity, according to [AVITRADER](https://avitrader.com/2026/07/14/embraer-launches-phenom-300ev/).

**Avionics and systems.** The Phenom 300EV features an upgraded Garmin G3000 Prodigy Touch flight deck with 3D taxiway routing, runway-occupancy alerts, synthetic-vision guidance, FANS 1/A+ datalink, RNP AR 0.3 approach capability, a new inertial reference system and autothrottle, according to [AVweb](https://avweb.com/aviation-news/embraer-phenom-300ev-emergency-autoland/). A new Embraer-developed Multi-Purpose Electronic Controller integrates rudder-by-wire functionality, according to [AVITRADER](https://avitrader.com/2026/07/14/embraer-launches-phenom-300ev/). The aircraft comes with factory-installed Gogo Galileo low-Earth-orbit satellite connectivity, with Starlink available as an aftermarket installation, according to [AVweb](https://avweb.com/aviation-news/embraer-phenom-300ev-emergency-autoland/).

**Cabin.** Updates include a vacuum lavatory, improved cabin temperature controls, and an air-ionization system, according to [AVweb](https://avweb.com/aviation-news/embraer-phenom-300ev-emergency-autoland/).

**Certification and deliveries.** Embraer is targeting triple certification from Brazil's ANAC, the FAA, and Europe's EASA in 2026, according to [Corporate Jet Investor](https://www.corporatejetinvestor.com/news/embraer-launches-phenom-300ev/), with initial deliveries expected in 2028, according to [AVweb](https://avweb.com/aviation-news/embraer-phenom-300ev-emergency-autoland/).

**Track record.** The Phenom 300 family has delivered more than 930 aircraft since December 2009, with Embraer delivering 72 units in 2025 and 35 in the first half of 2026, according to [Corporate Jet Investor](https://www.corporatejetinvestor.com/news/embraer-launches-phenom-300ev/).

**Executive comment.** Michael Amalfitano, President and CEO of Embraer Executive Jets, said, "With the new Phenom 300EV, we are raising the bar once again," according to [AVweb](https://avweb.com/aviation-news/embraer-phenom-300ev-emergency-autoland/). He added, "Through purposeful innovations combining enhanced safety technology, amplified performance, and thoughtful cabin refinements, we have introduced meaningful advancements that make flying more intuitive, seamless and enjoyable," according to [AeroXplorer](https://aeroxplorer.com/articles/embraer-unveils-phenom-300ev-with-garmin-autoland-system-for-enhanced-safety.php).

## What We Don't Know

Embraer has not disclosed international pricing outside the $13,995,000 U.S. list price, nor has it said whether current Phenom 300E owners will have any retrofit path to the new Multi-Purpose Electronic Controller or Autobrake system. The exact certification date within 2026 has not been finalized, and none of the reporting on the launch specifies where Embraer held the unveiling event.
