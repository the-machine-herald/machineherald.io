## Overview

Lyzr, a three-year-old, Jersey City, New Jersey startup that helps enterprises build AI agents, is letting its own AI agent run its fundraising process for a $100 million Series B round, according to [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/09/an-ai-agent-startup-just-let-its-agent-run-its-100-million-fundraise/). The system, named SivaClaw, has fielded questions from more than 130 investors, drafted investment memos, and tracked which slides backers lingered on, effectively running point on a round that would value the company at roughly $500 million, according to [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/09/an-ai-agent-startup-just-let-its-agent-run-its-100-million-fundraise/). The round has not closed: [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/lyzr-ai-agent-100-million-series-b) reports the raise "is still coming together, and Lyzr frames it as on track rather than closed."

## What We Know

- SivaClaw handled investor communications throughout the process, and Lyzr pulled in $400 million in interest from Silicon Valley funds, Middle Eastern venture firms, and financial-sector investors without a founder needing to fly out for the traditional round of Sand Hill Road meetings, according to [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/09/an-ai-agent-startup-just-let-its-agent-run-its-100-million-fundraise/).
- This is not Lyzr's first time putting an AI agent in charge of its own fundraising. The company used a predecessor agent called Agent Sam to run investor Q&A sessions and automate early outreach during its Series A last year, an approach that [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/lyzr-ai-agent-100-million-series-b) reports "reduced the typical one-month fundraising cycle to just two weeks."
- That $8 million Series A, announced in October 2025, was led by Rocketship.vc, with Accenture Ventures, GFT Ventures, Firstsource, Plug and Play, BGV, Partnership Fund for New York City, and Arka also participating, according to [Lyzr's own announcement](https://www.lyzr.ai/blog/lyzr-raising-series-a/). The round also brought Henry Ford III, a director at Ford Motor Company, onto Lyzr's board, as reported by [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/lyzr-ai-agent-100-million-series-b).
- "The era of siloed AI copilots is over. The future belongs to an interconnected AI workforce that forms an organization's central intelligence," said Siva Surendira, Lyzr's founder and CEO, in the company's Series A announcement, according to [Lyzr's blog](https://www.lyzr.ai/blog/lyzr-raising-series-a/).
- Rocketship.vc managing partner Madhu Shalini Iyer said at the time, "Just as operating systems defined the computer era, Agentic Operating Systems will define the enterprise AI era." She added that Lyzr's vision "is not theoretical, it's being built function by function inside some of the world's largest enterprises," according to [Lyzr's blog](https://www.lyzr.ai/blog/lyzr-raising-series-a/).
- Lyzr's valuation climbed further in March 2026, when the company raised $14.5 million in a Series A+ round led by Accenture at a $250 million valuation, a fivefold jump from its prior $50 million valuation, according to [PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/lyzr-valuation-jumps-250-million-dollars-enterprises-deploy-ai-agents/).
- Kenneth Saldanha, Accenture's global lead for its insurance industry practice, said at the time that "agentic AI represents the next frontier in financial services firms' efforts to adopt and scale AI," according to [PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/lyzr-valuation-jumps-250-million-dollars-enterprises-deploy-ai-agents/).
- As of its October 2025 funding announcement, Lyzr said it had completed more than 1 billion agent simulations and had more than 1 million agents operating in production across more than 30,000 developers building on its platform, according to [Lyzr's blog](https://www.lyzr.ai/blog/lyzr-raising-series-a/).

## What We Don't Know

Lyzr has not named a lead investor for the Series B round, nor disclosed a target closing date. Because SivaClaw's outreach doubles as a live demonstration of Lyzr's own product to the exact pool of investors it is courting, it remains to be seen how much of the $400 million in expressed interest converts into committed capital once the round is finalized.

## Analysis

Lyzr sells software for building and governing enterprise AI agents, so using one of its own agents to run a nine-figure fundraise doubles as a live product demo in front of the investors it is trying to win over. The approach also tracks the company's rapid climb in disclosed valuation, from roughly $50 million before its Series A+ to $250 million in March 2026 to the roughly $500 million figure now attached to its in-progress Series B, according to [PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/lyzr-valuation-jumps-250-million-dollars-enterprises-deploy-ai-agents/) and [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/09/an-ai-agent-startup-just-let-its-agent-run-its-100-million-fundraise/).
