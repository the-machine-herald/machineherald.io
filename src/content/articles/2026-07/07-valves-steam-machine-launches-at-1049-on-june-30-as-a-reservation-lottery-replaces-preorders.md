## Overview

Valve's revived Steam Machine has finally shipped. Both versions of the compact, cube-shaped SteamOS console were scheduled to launch on June 30, according to [Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/Steam-Machine-release-date-and-price-officially-confirmed.1326720.0.html), with the base model starting at $1,049, as [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2199144/valve-steam-machine-starts-at-1049-with-512gb-storage-and-no-controller/) reported. The launch closes a saga The Machine Herald has followed since March, when Valve [recommitted to a 2026 launch](/article/2026-03/10-valve-recommits-to-shipping-steam-machine-in-2026-as-ai-driven-memory-shortage-forces-pricing-rethink) while conceding that pricing was still unsettled amid a component crunch. That pricing is now fixed — and it lands well above what Valve says it wanted to charge.

## Pricing and How to Buy

The Steam Machine ships in four configurations. According to [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2199144/valve-steam-machine-starts-at-1049-with-512gb-storage-and-no-controller/), the 512GB model costs $1,049 without a controller and $1,128 bundled with one, while the 2TB model costs $1,349 on its own and $1,428 with a controller. The Steam Controller can also be bought separately for $99.99, [Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/Steam-Machine-release-date-and-price-officially-confirmed.1326720.0.html) noted.

Rather than a conventional first-come, first-served sale, Valve is using a randomized reservation lottery. As [Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/Steam-Machine-release-date-and-price-officially-confirmed.1326720.0.html) described it, buyers had to join a registration list before the June 25 cutoff, after which "Valve will randomly select buyers and notify them by email." [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2199144/valve-steam-machine-starts-at-1049-with-512gb-storage-and-no-controller/) reported that reservation signups closed June 25 at 1PM ET and that selected customers began receiving purchase emails starting June 29. To enter, a Steam account had to be in good standing and have made a purchase before April 27, with one reservation permitted per household.

Valve framed the lottery as a fairness measure. A conventional timed launch, the company told [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2199144/valve-steam-machine-starts-at-1049-with-512gb-storage-and-no-controller/), "tends to reward bots, people with fast internet connections" and similar advantages.

## What's Inside

The Steam Machine is a small cube that measures around six inches on each side and runs SteamOS, the same Linux-based operating system as the Steam Deck, according to [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2199144/valve-steam-machine-starts-at-1049-with-512gb-storage-and-no-controller/). At its core is a semi-custom AMD chip. Per [GamersNexus](https://gamersnexus.net/pre-built-pc/valve-steam-machine-review-gpu-cpu-benchmarks-steamos-test-thermals-noise-and-price), the CPU is a 6-core, 12-thread Zen 4 part with a maximum advertised boost clock of 4.8 GHz and a 30W TDP, paired with an RDNA 3 GPU carrying 28 compute units, a 2.45 GHz maximum sustained clock, a 110W TDP, and 8GB of GDDR6 video memory. The same review lists 16GB of DDR5-5600 system memory. Connectivity includes Bluetooth 5.3, Wi-Fi 6E, DisplayPort 1.4, and HDMI 2.0, [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2199144/valve-steam-machine-starts-at-1049-with-512gb-storage-and-no-controller/) reported.

## Why It Costs This Much

The $1,049 starting price is far above Valve's internal target. [XDA Developers](https://www.xda-developers.com/this-is-why-the-steam-machine-costs-1049-instead-of-750-valve-had-zero-leverage-with-ram-makers/) reported that the company had aimed for a starting price closer to $750 before a global memory shortage — driven largely by AI data-center demand for the same chips — pushed the figure roughly 30 percent higher.

Valve has been unusually candid about its lack of leverage with memory suppliers. Describing how it buys DRAM, a Valve representative told [GamersNexus](https://gamersnexus.net/pre-built-pc/valve-steam-machine-review-gpu-cpu-benchmarks-steamos-test-thermals-noise-and-price): "There's no contracts. There's nothing. Those guys, they give us a price every month or something and they say you can buy that many and it's 'yes' or 'no.' And if we say 'no,' then they never talk to us again."

## Critical Reception

Early reviews praised the hardware while questioning the value proposition. In a [review roundup, PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/steam-machine/review-roundup-hefty-pricetag) recorded a range of verdicts, with IGN scoring the machine 8 out of 10 and The Verge giving it 6 out of 10. The common thread across reviews was that the Steam Machine is capable and well-built, but its price is hard to justify against the significantly cheaper PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X for anyone who mainly wants a living-room game console.

## What We Don't Know

Valve has not disclosed how many units were available in the first reservation batch, nor how large the waitlist grew. The company has also not announced pricing or a firm release date for the Steam Frame, the standalone SteamOS VR headset revealed alongside the Steam Machine and the new Steam Controller; The Machine Herald reported in May that the Frame was [nearing launch](/article/2026-05/22-valves-steam-frame-nears-launch-as-steamos-manager-gains-initial-support-and-proton-11-arm64-beta-ships) as its software support matured. Whether the memory-driven price pressure that reshaped the Steam Machine will similarly affect the Frame remains unclear.
