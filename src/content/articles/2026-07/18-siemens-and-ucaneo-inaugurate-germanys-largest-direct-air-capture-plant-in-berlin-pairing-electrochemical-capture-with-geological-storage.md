## Overview

Berlin-based climate tech startup Ucaneo and industrial conglomerate Siemens inaugurated Germany's largest direct air capture (DAC) facility on July 2, with [Siemens](https://press.siemens.com/global/en/pressrelease/siemens-and-ucaneo-partner-scale-direct-air-capture) confirming the plant carries a nameplate capacity of 150 metric tons of CO2 per year. The facility, located in Berlin's Marzahn district according to [H2 Bulletin](https://h2bulletin.com/ucaneo-inaugurates-germanys-largest-direct-air-capture-plant-in-berlin/), is also Germany's first verified direct air capture project combined with permanent geological storage, as [Siemens](https://press.siemens.com/global/en/pressrelease/siemens-and-ucaneo-partner-scale-direct-air-capture) and [Carbon Herald](https://carbonherald.com/ucaneo-opens-germanys-largest-dac-plant/) both report.

## What We Know

- The plant uses an electrochemical DAC process, inspired by the human lung, that removes CO2 directly from ambient air and delivers high-purity CO2 at over 99.9 percent purity, according to [Carbon Herald](https://carbonherald.com/ucaneo-opens-germanys-largest-dac-plant/).
- The process is fully electrified and requires zero thermal energy, allowing it to run its capture loops flexibly alongside fluctuating renewable power generation and grid pricing, as [Process Worldwide](https://www.process-worldwide.com/ucaneo-inaugurates-germanys-largest-dac-facility-a-91443e483c1f5e73a90a0052f81d4a75/) reports.
- Part of the captured CO2 will be permanently stored in geological formations, while the remainder can be used as a feedstock for climate-neutral products including sustainable aviation fuel, methanol, and food and beverage applications, according to [Siemens](https://press.siemens.com/global/en/pressrelease/siemens-and-ucaneo-partner-scale-direct-air-capture).
- A follow-on commercial plant with roughly ten times the capacity of the Marzahn installation is already in development, with construction due to start next year, [Chemeurope](https://www.chemeurope.com/en/news/1189062/siemens-and-ucaneo-partner-to-scale-direct-air-capture.html) and [H2 Bulletin](https://h2bulletin.com/ucaneo-inaugurates-germanys-largest-direct-air-capture-plant-in-berlin/) both report.
- Ucaneo is targeting the annual capture of half a gigaton of CO2 from the atmosphere by 2035, an amount [Siemens](https://press.siemens.com/global/en/pressrelease/siemens-and-ucaneo-partner-scale-direct-air-capture) says is roughly equivalent to canceling out the entire annual CO2 emissions of Canada.
- Founded in Berlin in 2022 by Carla Glassl and Florian Tiller, Ucaneo now employs around 30 people and has raised approximately €17 million to date, backed by investors including Aramco Ventures, the Grantham Foundation, Nucleus Capital, and public funding from the German federal government, according to [H2 Bulletin](https://h2bulletin.com/ucaneo-inaugurates-germanys-largest-direct-air-capture-plant-in-berlin/). That total includes a €6.75 million seed round announced in September 2024, per [Aramco Ventures](https://aramcoventures.com/news/aramco-ventures-joins-ucaneos-seed-funding-round-to-build-germanys-largest-direct-air-capture/), which says Ucaneo's technology has the potential to bring costs below €300 per ton of CO2 in the near future.
- Under the partnership, Siemens is deploying its Xcelerator portfolio as Ucaneo's "preferred automation and digitalization partner," providing what [Siemens](https://press.siemens.com/global/en/pressrelease/siemens-and-ucaneo-partner-scale-direct-air-capture) describes as comprehensive support across process automation, instrumentation and analytics, drive technology, and digitalization from the first-of-a-kind plant through commercial scale-up.
- "What Ucaneo has built is exactly what the energy transition needs: a Direct Air Capture process that is efficient, fully electrified, and ready for industrial scale," said Christian Gückel, Head of Vertical Chemicals at Siemens Digital Industries, according to [Siemens](https://press.siemens.com/global/en/pressrelease/siemens-and-ucaneo-partner-scale-direct-air-capture).
- "Building the carbon economy requires scalable and reliable infrastructure. Siemens is a key partner in helping us standardize and automate DAC systems," said Florian Tiller, Co-founder and CEO of Ucaneo, per [Siemens](https://press.siemens.com/global/en/pressrelease/siemens-and-ucaneo-partner-scale-direct-air-capture).

## What We Don't Know

Neither Siemens nor Ucaneo has disclosed the exact site or capacity of the geological storage location where captured CO2 will be sequestered, nor a firm construction start date for the follow-on plant beyond "next year." The precise current cost per ton of CO2 removed at the Marzahn facility has not been published; Aramco Ventures describes only a forward-looking target of below €300 per ton.

## Analysis

The Berlin plant is a small facility in absolute terms — 150 metric tons of CO2 per year is a fraction of the output from megascale DAC projects elsewhere, including the gigaton-scale ambitions [previously reported](/article/2026-03/22-carbon-capture-enters-its-commercial-era-as-first-megascale-dac-and-ccs-plants-near-operations) across the direct air capture industry. Its significance lies less in scale than in structure: it is the first project in Germany to pair verified direct air capture with permanent geological storage, giving Ucaneo a domestic reference site to validate the electrochemical technology before its planned tenfold scale-up. The Siemens partnership, which extends beyond this single site to a standardized automation template intended for global rollout, signals that an established industrial automation supplier sees direct air capture as mature enough to warrant dedicated commercial tooling rather than one-off engineering.
