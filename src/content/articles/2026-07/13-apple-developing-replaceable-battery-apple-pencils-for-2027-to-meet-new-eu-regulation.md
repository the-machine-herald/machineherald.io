## Overview

Apple is developing new Apple Pencil models with easier-to-replace batteries, according to a report from Bloomberg's Mark Gurman cited by [9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/07/12/apple-pencil-ipad-new-battery-replacement/), with the redesigned styluses expected to launch alongside new iPad Pro hardware in 2027. The changes are aimed at complying with an EU rule requiring that "portable batteries are readily removable and replaceable by the end user, during the lifetime of the product," according to [9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/07/12/apple-pencil-ipad-new-battery-replacement/).

## What We Know

- According to [9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/07/12/apple-pencil-ipad-new-battery-replacement/), citing Bloomberg's Mark Gurman, "Apple is developing new Apple Pencil models launching alongside iPad Pro devices in spring 2027," with new battery systems "which would make them more easily user-replaceable in order to comply with upcoming EU regulation."
- [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2213077/refreshed-apple-pencils-may-arrive-next-year-with-improved-repairability/) reports Apple is working on two specific models: an entry-level Apple Pencil update with USB-C charging, internally codenamed B582, and a refreshed Apple Pencil Pro, codenamed B632, both targeted for the first half of 2027.
- [AppleInsider](https://appleinsider.com/articles/26/07/12/eu-regulations-could-force-an-apple-pencil-upgrade-in-early-2027), citing Bloomberg's "Power On" newsletter, similarly reports that "Apple is expected to release an updated Apple Pencil Pro and Apple Pencil with USB-C in early 2027," alongside the iPad Pro with M6 that AppleInsider says "is expected in early 2027."
- The regulation driving the redesign is Article 11 of the EU Batteries Regulation, which [9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/07/12/apple-pencil-ipad-new-battery-replacement/) says "came into effect in 2023 but is not legally applicable until February 2027." [AppleInsider](https://appleinsider.com/articles/26/07/12/eu-regulations-could-force-an-apple-pencil-upgrade-in-early-2027) similarly notes "the European Union mandated in 2023 that consumer electronics must have easily replaced batteries by 2027."
- Per [9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/07/12/apple-pencil-ipad-new-battery-replacement/), the rule's scope extends beyond phones to "accessories like rechargeable styli, keyboard and mice, headphones and more."
- Both [9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/07/12/apple-pencil-ipad-new-battery-replacement/) and [AppleInsider](https://appleinsider.com/articles/26/07/12/eu-regulations-could-force-an-apple-pencil-upgrade-in-early-2027) describe the current Apple Pencil in identical terms: "a hunk of plastic filled with glue" that is "virtually impossible to repair."
- [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2213077/refreshed-apple-pencils-may-arrive-next-year-with-improved-repairability/) notes that iFixit has separately rated all existing Apple Pencil models "repairability fails" because their internal batteries cannot be swapped once they degrade.
- [9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/07/12/apple-pencil-ipad-new-battery-replacement/) reports that smartphones such as iPhones "may already comply through Apple's Self Service Repair program," while AirPods present a harder case: "there isn't an obvious way Apple could manufacture miniaturized wireless earbuds that allow for the battery to be easily replaced."
- [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2213077/refreshed-apple-pencils-may-arrive-next-year-with-improved-repairability/) points to precedent, noting Apple previously "switched to USB-C on the iPhone 15 lineup due to similar regulatory pressure" from the EU.

## What We Don't Know

None of the reports specify what new features, if any, the redesigned Apple Pencils will include beyond battery replaceability; [AppleInsider](https://appleinsider.com/articles/26/07/12/eu-regulations-could-force-an-apple-pencil-upgrade-in-early-2027) notes the update "could simply be focused around the battery change" rather than new capabilities. Reporting also varies on the precise launch window, describing it as "spring 2027," "early 2027" and "the first half of 2027" across the three outlets. Pricing for the new styluses, the exact construction change needed to remove the internal adhesive, and how Apple might eventually address battery replaceability in more miniaturized accessories such as AirPods remain unaddressed in current reporting.

## Analysis

The Apple Pencil overhaul would make Apple the latest major electronics maker to redesign a flagship accessory around the EU's removable-battery mandate. Nintendo has [previously moved](/article/2026-07/08-nintendo-to-sell-a-switch-2-with-a-user-replaceable-battery-in-europe-this-autumn-as-eu-rules-end-original-switch-sales-in-february-2027) to introduce a Switch 2 hardware revision with a user-replaceable battery in Europe under the same regulation. Where Nintendo's fix required only modest trade-offs to battery capacity and weight, Apple's challenge looks different: the Pencil line, according to both [9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/07/12/apple-pencil-ipad-new-battery-replacement/) and [AppleInsider](https://appleinsider.com/articles/26/07/12/eu-regulations-could-force-an-apple-pencil-upgrade-in-early-2027), is currently held together with internal adhesive rather than screws or seams, suggesting compliance may require a more fundamental construction change than a simple hardware revision.
