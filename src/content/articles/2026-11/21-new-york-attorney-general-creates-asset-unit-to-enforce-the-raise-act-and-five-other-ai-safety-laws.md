## Overview

New York Attorney General Letitia James announced on November 17 the creation of a new unit inside the Office of the Attorney General (OAG) dedicated to enforcing the state's AI safety laws, according to an [official OAG press release](https://ag.ny.gov/press-release/2026/attorney-general-james-announces-creation-ai-safety-standards-enforcement-and). The AI Safety, Standards, Enforcement, and Transparency (ASSET) Unit will lead OAG's enforcement of New York's Responsible AI Safety Education (RAISE) Act, which takes effect January 1, 2027, along with five other state laws touching AI-related harms, according to [the release](https://ag.ny.gov/press-release/2026/attorney-general-james-announces-creation-ai-safety-standards-enforcement-and).

## What We Know

According to the [OAG announcement](https://ag.ny.gov/press-release/2026/attorney-general-james-announces-creation-ai-safety-standards-enforcement-and), the ASSET Unit will "lead OAG's enforcement of the RAISE Act and other AI safety laws, develop new policy proposals, and lend technical expertise to assist investigations throughout OAG." The unit sits within OAG's Economic Justice Division and will work closely with the office's Bureau of Internet and Technology, per [the release](https://ag.ny.gov/press-release/2026/attorney-general-james-announces-creation-ai-safety-standards-enforcement-and). OAG says it is still building out the ASSET Unit's team, which will be led by Cristy Phillips, previously Special Counsel for the Economic Justice Division, according to [the announcement](https://ag.ny.gov/press-release/2026/attorney-general-james-announces-creation-ai-safety-standards-enforcement-and).

Beyond the RAISE Act, the [OAG release](https://ag.ny.gov/press-release/2026/attorney-general-james-announces-creation-ai-safety-standards-enforcement-and) lists five other state laws the unit will help enforce: the SHIELD Act, which requires reasonable data security practices; S.9051B/A.10379C, a recently signed law banning unsafe chatbot features for children that takes effect in June 2027; General Business Law Article 47, which requires AI chatbots to make reasonable efforts to detect and address suicidal ideation and to disclose that users aren't talking to a human; New York Penal Law section 245.15, which criminalizes distributing intimate images with intent to harm, including AI-altered images; and the Synthetic Performer Disclosure Act, which requires companies to disclose when AI is used in place of humans in advertisements.

"Promising new technologies like artificial intelligence should never come at the expense of New Yorkers' safety and privacy," James said, according to [the OAG release](https://ag.ny.gov/press-release/2026/attorney-general-james-announces-creation-ai-safety-standards-enforcement-and). "My office has always stepped up to enforce the law to protect New Yorkers when big tech companies try to take advantage of them. Our ASSET Unit will lead the way in ensuring AI and other emerging technology are developed safely and for the benefit of all New Yorkers."

The [OAG release](https://ag.ny.gov/press-release/2026/attorney-general-james-announces-creation-ai-safety-standards-enforcement-and) also points to several prior actions it says the new unit builds on: in September 2026, James called on Congress to address unchecked AI development and urged AI industry employees to file whistleblower complaints with her office; James separately issued a consumer alert about scammers using AI-generated fakes of celebrities to lure investors into scams; and in January 2026, James demanded that xAI do more to stop its Grok chatbot from generating inappropriate images.

## Context

The RAISE Act that the new unit will enforce requires large frontier AI developers — companies with more than $500 million in annual revenue — to publish safety frameworks on their websites, report critical safety incidents within 72 hours, and issue public model transparency reports, according to [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/10/openai-anthropic-face-new-oversight-as-new-yorks-ai-safety-law-takes-effect/). The law is implemented by a separate state office, the Office of Digital Innovation, Governance, Integrity, and Trust (DIGIT), which opened an online registration portal on November 10 requiring large frontier developers such as Anthropic and OpenAI to register by January 1, 2027, [TechCrunch reported](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/10/openai-anthropic-face-new-oversight-as-new-yorks-ai-safety-law-takes-effect/). Penalties for violations can reach $1 million for a first offense and $3 million for subsequent violations, and the state can also bring enforcement actions against companies that fail to register at all, according to [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/10/openai-anthropic-face-new-oversight-as-new-yorks-ai-safety-law-takes-effect/).

New York [finalized the RAISE Act in April](/article/2026-04/21-new-york-finalizes-the-raise-act-setting-the-strictest-frontier-ai-transparency-rules-in-the-united-states), and the registration mechanics took a concrete step forward on November 10, the same day Governor Kathy Hochul launched the New York Center for AI Responsibility and Research at Binghamton University, according to the [Governor's office](https://www.governor.ny.gov/news/governor-hochul-declares-ny-will-lead-nation-ai-science-and-safety-launches-new-research). That office confirmed the DIGIT portal's registration deadline of January 1, 2027, and the RAISE Act's 72-hour critical-incident reporting requirement, which it called the "strongest-in-the-nation" among comparable state laws, according to the [Governor's release](https://www.governor.ny.gov/news/governor-hochul-declares-ny-will-lead-nation-ai-science-and-safety-launches-new-research).

## What's Next

OAG has not specified how many staff will join the ASSET Unit or when it will reach full strength, according to [the release announcing it](https://ag.ny.gov/press-release/2026/attorney-general-james-announces-creation-ai-safety-standards-enforcement-and). Large frontier AI developers still have until January 1, 2027, to register with DIGIT and comply with the RAISE Act's disclosure and reporting requirements, while the child-focused chatbot law the unit will also enforce does not take effect until June 2027, according to [the OAG release](https://ag.ny.gov/press-release/2026/attorney-general-james-announces-creation-ai-safety-standards-enforcement-and).
