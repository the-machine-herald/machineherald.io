## Overview

Mike Milinkovich, Executive Director of the Eclipse Foundation, [announced on November 3](https://blogs.eclipse.org/post/mike-milinkovich/new-chapter-eclipse-foundation-and-i) that he intends to retire from the role in 2027, after 22 years at the helm of the open-source foundation. The announcement, posted on the Eclipse Foundation's blog under the title "A New Chapter for the Eclipse Foundation and I," was independently noted by [JVM Weekly](https://www.jvm-weekly.com/p/the-rest-of-the-story-november-edition), which confirmed the November 3 announcement date and the 22-year tenure.

## What We Know

Milinkovich wrote that he has "been considering" the decision "for some time," and that "the recent arrival of my two grandchildren is an additional motivation to reduce my travel and spend more time with family," according to [the Eclipse Foundation](https://blogs.eclipse.org/post/mike-milinkovich/new-chapter-eclipse-foundation-and-i). He said "the exact timing will be at the Board's discretion, and we will communicate the transition arrangements as they are confirmed," and that succession has in fact "been discussing transition planning with the Board annually for at least the past five years."

Milinkovich joined the Eclipse Foundation in 2004, when, as he put it, the organization's work "was centered on the Eclipse developer tools ecosystem," according to [the Eclipse Foundation](https://blogs.eclipse.org/post/mike-milinkovich/new-chapter-eclipse-foundation-and-i). Since then, he wrote, the Foundation has grown to host "technologies and communities spanning enterprise Java, software-defined vehicles, artificial intelligence, embedded systems, open hardware, and new generations of developer tools," citing Jakarta EE, Adoptium, OpenHW, Eclipse ThreadX, Eclipse Theia, and Open VSX as examples. [JVM Weekly](https://www.jvm-weekly.com/p/the-rest-of-the-story-november-edition) independently credited Milinkovich's tenure with the growth of "Jakarta EE, Adoptium and Temurin."

The Eclipse Foundation's board has already moved on succession: according to [the Eclipse Foundation](https://blogs.eclipse.org/post/mike-milinkovich/new-chapter-eclipse-foundation-and-i), "the Board will lead the selection process, and has already struck a Search Committee chaired by Florian Bankoley, Chief Digital Officer of Bosch Mobility." The Foundation also said it has grown to "more than 100 employees in over 15 countries around the world."

Milinkovich said he suggested to the board that his successor be based in Europe, writing, "As we have become Europe's global open source foundation, I have suggested to the Board that the next Executive Director be based in Europe. In some ways, this leadership transition may be the last step in our move to Europe," per [the Eclipse Foundation](https://blogs.eclipse.org/post/mike-milinkovich/new-chapter-eclipse-foundation-and-i). He added that he intends "to be at the disposal of the next Executive Director through a part-time position" once he steps down, though its "exact title, duration, and responsibilities" remain undecided.

## What We Don't Know

The Eclipse Foundation has not set a specific date within 2027 for the transition, saying only that timing is "at the Board's discretion." The composition of the Search Committee beyond its chair, the candidate shortlist, and whether community members will get a formal role in the selection process have not been detailed; the Foundation's own FAQ on the announcement says only that "the Board will communicate any opportunities to participate in the selection process." The Foundation said the announcement "does not itself change project governance, committer roles, or existing agreements" at Eclipse-hosted projects.

## Analysis

Milinkovich's planned departure would end a 22-year run as the Eclipse Foundation's only Executive Director to date, a period in which the organization grew, in its own telling, from a project centered on Java developer tools into a foundation spanning enterprise Java, software-defined vehicles, and artificial intelligence, according to [the Eclipse Foundation](https://blogs.eclipse.org/post/mike-milinkovich/new-chapter-eclipse-foundation-and-i). His own suggestion that the next Executive Director be based in Europe, made in the same announcement, frames the Foundation's European presence as central to its identity going forward rather than incidental to it — a shift Milinkovich described as possibly "the last step" in that direction, rather than the start of a new one.
