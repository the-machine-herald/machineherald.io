## Overview

Federal Reserve Chairman Kevin Warsh opened the first hearing of a Fed task force dedicated to artificial intelligence's economic effects on Monday, November 16, telling attendees in San Francisco that the technology "will have significant impact on the economy, with important implications for monetary policy down the road," according to [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/16/fed-ai-task-force-kevin-warsh.html) and [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/economy/policy/article/warsh-kicks-off-first-task-force-hearing-on-how-ai-could-fuel-growth-or-disrupt-jobs-171720426.html). The hearing, held at a conference hosted by the San Francisco Federal Reserve, was the first for one of five task forces Warsh has established since becoming Fed chairman, according to [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/economy/policy/article/warsh-kicks-off-first-task-force-hearing-on-how-ai-could-fuel-growth-or-disrupt-jobs-171720426.html); the other four address Fed communication, data reliance, balance-sheet policy and inflation frameworks, according to [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/16/fed-ai-task-force-kevin-warsh.html).

## What We Know

Marc Andreessen, co-founder of venture capital firm Andreessen Horowitz, is the task force's leader and joined Warsh for a closing fireside chat at Monday's conference, according to [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/16/fed-ai-task-force-kevin-warsh.html). Other members assembled by Warsh include Asha Sharma, Microsoft executive vice president and Xbox CEO, and Charles Jones, a Stanford University economics professor currently on leave at Anthropic, according to [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/economy/policy/article/warsh-kicks-off-first-task-force-hearing-on-how-ai-could-fuel-growth-or-disrupt-jobs-171720426.html).

On a panel moderated by Warsh, Nikesh Arora, chief executive of Palo Alto Networks, said job displacement from AI is still some way off because the technology remains "still early to give it arms and legs" — meaning, he said, "giving an agency or giving it independent authority to go act on our behalf, and I think that's where the fear is, because that's where the job displacement begins," according to [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/economy/policy/article/warsh-kicks-off-first-task-force-hearing-on-how-ai-could-fuel-growth-or-disrupt-jobs-171720426.html). Stripe founder Patrick Collison told the same panel that "the US is in a definitively preeminent position today," adding, "we see it directly in our data: AI usage intensity is higher in the US than elsewhere. An overwhelming majority of the revenue and the firms and the creativity and the innovation is occurring in the US generally and indeed in the Bay Area specifically," according to [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/economy/policy/article/warsh-kicks-off-first-task-force-hearing-on-how-ai-could-fuel-growth-or-disrupt-jobs-171720426.html).

In his own remarks, Warsh laid out three possible economic scenarios for AI, according to [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/economy/policy/article/warsh-kicks-off-first-task-force-hearing-on-how-ai-could-fuel-growth-or-disrupt-jobs-171720426.html) and [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/16/fed-ai-task-force-kevin-warsh.html). In the first, productivity and economic gains are shared broadly across labor and capital, with inflation-adjusted GDP growth rising persistently above its long-run average while the US stays close to full employment, according to [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/economy/policy/article/warsh-kicks-off-first-task-force-hearing-on-how-ai-could-fuel-growth-or-disrupt-jobs-171720426.html). In the second, AI displaces jobs rather than complementing workers; GDP growth would still exceed its long-term average, but widespread automation could push unemployment higher, according to [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/economy/policy/article/warsh-kicks-off-first-task-force-hearing-on-how-ai-could-fuel-growth-or-disrupt-jobs-171720426.html). In the third, Warsh said AI could simply "disappoint": "After significant capital investment and stock market gains, expected returns could turn down and equity valuations could fall, leading to an economic slowdown and a more notable rise in unemployment," he said. "In time, the economy would recover, but without the productivity gains," according to [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/16/fed-ai-task-force-kevin-warsh.html).

Arora told the panel he puts the odds of the positive scenario at 98%, and Warsh said he considers it more likely that AI will spread across the US economy faster than anywhere else, translating into efficiency gains, higher profit margins and greater global competitiveness, according to [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/economy/policy/article/warsh-kicks-off-first-task-force-hearing-on-how-ai-could-fuel-growth-or-disrupt-jobs-171720426.html).

Warsh also used the term "super intelligence" rather than "artificial intelligence" in his prepared remarks, which CNBC noted follows a recent Trump administration directive for federal agencies to favor that term, according to [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/16/fed-ai-task-force-kevin-warsh.html). Monday's visit was the first Warsh has made to a regional Fed bank since becoming chairman, according to [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/16/fed-ai-task-force-kevin-warsh.html).

## What We Don't Know

Neither of the cited sources reports a timeline for when the task force's conclusions will feed into Fed interest-rate or policy decisions, or whether its findings carry any binding weight on the central bank's actions.
