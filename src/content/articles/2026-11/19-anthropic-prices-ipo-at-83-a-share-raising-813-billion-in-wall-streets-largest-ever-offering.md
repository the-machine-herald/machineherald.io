## Background

Anthropic's IPO follows a private fundraising run that pushed its valuation from $965 billion in a May 2026 Series H round to a reported target of $2 trillion in the months that followed, a target the public offering did not reach, according to [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/18/anthropic-reportedly-prices-ipo-at-83-a-share-above-projected-range/). The company's most recent amended registration statement was filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission on November 17, 2026, by Anthropic, PBC, a Delaware-incorporated public benefit corporation based in San Francisco, according to [SEC EDGAR](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2130357/000162828026074338/0001628280-26-074338-index.htm). Going into the offering, Anthropic's major venture backers included Sequoia, Spark Capital, Iconiq, Menlo Ventures, Coatue, and Sound Ventures, while strategic partners Amazon and Google held approximately 15% and 14% stakes, respectively, according to [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/18/anthropic-reportedly-prices-ipo-at-83-a-share-above-projected-range/). CNBC separately reported that Amazon and Google each hold stakes valued at more than $100 billion, without specifying the underlying percentage, according to [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/18/anthropic-ipo-ai-offering-price.html).

Anthropic's chief rival, OpenAI, was last valued at $1.25 trillion privately and has no announced IPO plans, according to [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/18/anthropic-reportedly-prices-ipo-at-83-a-share-above-projected-range/). OpenAI is holding off on its own IPO until at least 2027, which Fortune says makes Anthropic the first "pure-play" maker of frontier AI models to go public, according to [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/11/18/anthropic-prices-ipo-83-per-share-1-5-trillion/).

## Current situation

Anthropic priced its IPO at $83 per share on November 18, 2026, above its initial range of $70 to $78, according to [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/18/anthropic-reportedly-prices-ipo-at-83-a-share-above-projected-range/) and [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/11/18/anthropic-prices-ipo-83-per-share-1-5-trillion/). Anthropic also increased the number of shares sold in the offering to 980 million, up from the 825 million shares it had initially indicated it would sell, according to [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/11/18/anthropic-prices-ipo-83-per-share-1-5-trillion/). The larger, higher-priced sale generated $81.3 billion in proceeds, which TechCrunch and CNBC both describe as the largest IPO in history by proceeds raised, surpassing the $75 billion SpaceX raised in its own offering, according to [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/18/anthropic-reportedly-prices-ipo-at-83-a-share-above-projected-range/) and [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/18/anthropic-ipo-ai-offering-price.html). CNBC noted that SpaceX's total climbed to $85.7 billion after underwriters exercised overallotment options, according to [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/18/anthropic-ipo-ai-offering-price.html). At the IPO price, Anthropic is valued at approximately $1.5 trillion on a fully diluted basis, according to [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/18/anthropic-reportedly-prices-ipo-at-83-a-share-above-projected-range/), [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/18/anthropic-ipo-ai-offering-price.html), and [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/11/18/anthropic-prices-ipo-83-per-share-1-5-trillion/) — a figure Fortune ranks as the second-largest IPO valuation ever, behind SpaceX's, even as Anthropic's offering raised more capital, according to [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/11/18/anthropic-prices-ipo-83-per-share-1-5-trillion/). Trading in Anthropic shares, under the Nasdaq ticker "ANT," was set to begin Thursday, according to [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/18/anthropic-reportedly-prices-ipo-at-83-a-share-above-projected-range/) and [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/18/anthropic-ipo-ai-offering-price.html).

Separate from the public share sale, a technology consortium including AMD, Broadcom, Google, Micron, Microsoft, Nvidia, and TSMC Partners agreed to purchase $26 billion in non-voting shares at the IPO price, according to [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/18/anthropic-reportedly-prices-ipo-at-83-a-share-above-projected-range/). Each of Anthropic's seven co-founders, including CEO Dario Amodei, President Daniella Amodei, and Chief Science Officer Jared Kaplan, sold shares in the offering, according to [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/11/18/anthropic-prices-ipo-83-per-share-1-5-trillion/) and [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/18/anthropic-reportedly-prices-ipo-at-83-a-share-above-projected-range/); TechCrunch reports each sold roughly 3.4 million shares, netting approximately $282 million individually, according to [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/18/anthropic-reportedly-prices-ipo-at-83-a-share-above-projected-range/).

On the financial disclosures that accompanied the offering, Anthropic reported $33.24 billion in revenue for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, up roughly 1,200% from the same period a year earlier, and said its run-rate revenue reached roughly $81 billion at the end of October, up $10 billion from the previous month, according to [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/18/anthropic-ipo-ai-offering-price.html). Over the same nine-month period, the company posted a $104 billion net loss, which Fortune reports was driven largely by how Anthropic accounted for its convertible notes rather than by operating losses, according to [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/11/18/anthropic-prices-ipo-83-per-share-1-5-trillion/).

## Implications

TechCrunch frames Anthropic's market debut as a test of investor confidence in AI adoption at a moment of recent volatility affecting AI-focused stocks, and says the stock's performance may set expectations for other private AI companies considering a public listing, particularly OpenAI, according to [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/18/anthropic-reportedly-prices-ipo-at-83-a-share-above-projected-range/). Fortune places the offering in a similarly uncertain moment for the sector, noting that it comes after a leadership shake-up at Nvidia and amid growing public backlash to AI data centers, according to [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/11/18/anthropic-prices-ipo-83-per-share-1-5-trillion/).

Morningstar analyst Malik Ahmed Khan said Anthropic "will need to sustain rapid growth while managing the enormous cost of developing and running its models, and there is still considerable uncertainty around how the competitive landscape and economics of generative AI will evolve," according to [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/11/18/anthropic-prices-ipo-83-per-share-1-5-trillion/). Khan added that his valuation of the company "reflects the significant growth we expect from AI adoption and Anthropic's position at the frontier of model development, particularly in areas such as coding and cybersecurity," according to [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/11/18/anthropic-prices-ipo-83-per-share-1-5-trillion/).

The $26 billion non-voting investment from AMD, Broadcom, Google, Micron, Microsoft, Nvidia, and TSMC Partners links Anthropic's public debut directly to the compute supply chain it depends on: several of the same companies that build the chips, cloud capacity, and manufacturing Anthropic needs to keep scaling its models are now also shareholders in its public stock, according to [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/18/anthropic-reportedly-prices-ipo-at-83-a-share-above-projected-range/). Whether that alignment of supplier and shareholder incentives proves durable, and whether Anthropic's revenue growth can continue to outpace the model-development and infrastructure costs Khan flagged, are questions the sources reviewed here do not resolve; they will instead play out in Anthropic's public financial disclosures as a listed company.
