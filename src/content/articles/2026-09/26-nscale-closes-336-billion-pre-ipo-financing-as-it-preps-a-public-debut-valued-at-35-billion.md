## Overview

British AI infrastructure company Nscale has secured [$3.36 billion in convertible financing](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/25/ahead-of-u-s-ipo-british-ai-neocloud-nscale-secures-3-36b-in-convertible-finacing/) as it prepares for a New York Stock Exchange listing, [led by hedge fund Third Point](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/25/ahead-of-u-s-ipo-british-ai-neocloud-nscale-secures-3-36b-in-convertible-finacing/). Of that total, [$2.36 billion is available to the company immediately, with an additional $1 billion coming from existing investor Nvidia in mid-November](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/25/ahead-of-u-s-ipo-british-ai-neocloud-nscale-secures-3-36b-in-convertible-finacing/). The round arrives as [Nscale, which filed its IPO paperwork the prior week, is expected to be valued at $35 billion on the NYSE](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/25/ahead-of-u-s-ipo-british-ai-neocloud-nscale-secures-3-36b-in-convertible-finacing/) and [is seeking to raise $3 billion in the offering](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/22/nscales-ipo-will-test-wall-streets-appetite-for-concentrated-ai-bets-once-again/).

Nscale [previously raised $2 billion in a Series C round](/article/2026-03/11-nscale-raises-2-billion-in-largest-european-series-c-as-nvidia-citadel-and-jane-street-back-ai-infrastructure-expansion) in March 2026 at a $14.6 billion valuation. The new convertible notes will [convert into shares immediately after the planned IPO](https://siliconangle.com/2026/09/25/ai-data-center-builder-nscale-raises-3-36b-ahead-of-ipo/).

## What We Know

Beyond Third Point, the consortium behind the immediate tranche includes [Apollo, Citadel and more than a dozen other institutional investors](https://siliconangle.com/2026/09/25/ai-data-center-builder-nscale-raises-3-36b-ahead-of-ipo/). Nscale [was spun out of Australian cryptocurrency mining company Arkon Energy two years ago](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/25/ahead-of-u-s-ipo-british-ai-neocloud-nscale-secures-3-36b-in-convertible-finacing/) and is [currently developing large data center campuses including in Norway and West Virginia](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/25/ahead-of-u-s-ipo-british-ai-neocloud-nscale-secures-3-36b-in-convertible-finacing/). The West Virginia site is [an AI campus with an on-site microgrid capable of generating 2 gigawatts of power when it comes online in early 2028](https://siliconangle.com/2026/09/25/ai-data-center-builder-nscale-raises-3-36b-ahead-of-ipo/), and according to the company [it won't draw any electricity from the public grid](https://siliconangle.com/2026/09/25/ai-data-center-builder-nscale-raises-3-36b-ahead-of-ipo/). Nscale's [data center network includes 16 other sites, five of them company-owned and the rest operated by partners](https://siliconangle.com/2026/09/25/ai-data-center-builder-nscale-raises-3-36b-ahead-of-ipo/).

"This marks a milestone for Nscale as we continue scaling our full-stack AI infrastructure to meet unprecedented global demand," said [Nscale founder and Chief Executive Officer Josh Payne](https://siliconangle.com/2026/09/25/ai-data-center-builder-nscale-raises-3-36b-ahead-of-ipo/).

Ahead of the listing, Nscale has also been building out its board and disclosing its finances for the first time. Earlier this month, the company [added Fidji Simo, formerly CEO of AGI deployment at OpenAI and essentially the No. 2 executive at the AI lab](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/11/nscale-adds-former-openai-exec-fidji-simo-to-its-board-ahead-of-potential-ipo/), who [left OpenAI in July citing health reasons](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/11/nscale-adds-former-openai-exec-fidji-simo-to-its-board-ahead-of-potential-ipo/). She [joins existing board members Sheryl Sandberg, Susan Decker and Nick Clegg](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/11/nscale-adds-former-openai-exec-fidji-simo-to-its-board-ahead-of-potential-ipo/). "Fidji joins a board we've built deliberately, with the experience of scaling platforms to the largest in the world, and the independence, financial rigor and operating depth to which companies at this scale are held," Payne said in [announcing her appointment](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/11/nscale-adds-former-openai-exec-fidji-simo-to-its-board-ahead-of-potential-ipo/).

Nscale's IPO filing shows the company has [amassed over $103 billion worth of contracts](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/25/ahead-of-u-s-ipo-british-ai-neocloud-nscale-secures-3-36b-in-convertible-finacing/), but that revenue is highly concentrated: [about 85% comes from a deal to supply Microsoft with $43.8 billion worth of compute through 2033, and another supply agreement worth $44.6 billion with Anthropic](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/22/nscales-ipo-will-test-wall-streets-appetite-for-concentrated-ai-bets-once-again/). Notably, [Anthropic's agreement is contingent on Nscale obtaining financing, and the AI lab retains the right to walk away from or cancel the deal if Nscale fails to hit milestones the filing categorizes as "stringent"](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/22/nscales-ipo-will-test-wall-streets-appetite-for-concentrated-ai-bets-once-again/). The filing also disclosed that [Nscale reported revenue of $140.6 million for the six months ended June 30, up from $10.4 million a year earlier, while net losses jumped to $1.02 billion from $369 million in the same period](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/22/nscales-ipo-will-test-wall-streets-appetite-for-concentrated-ai-bets-once-again/).

## What We Don't Know

Nscale has not disclosed an exact IPO listing date beyond the general expectation of a debut this fall. It is also unclear how the $2.36 billion immediate tranche will be allocated across the company's data center buildout versus other uses, or how the "stringent" milestones tied to the Anthropic contract will be measured and reported to investors.
