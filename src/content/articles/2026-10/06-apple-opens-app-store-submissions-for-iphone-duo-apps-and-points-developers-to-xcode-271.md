## Overview

Apple has opened App Store submissions for apps optimized for the iPhone Duo, its first foldable phone. According to [Apple Developer News](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=kkphp5qo), the device becomes available to customers on October 23, 2026, and developers are directed to recompile existing apps with Xcode 27.1, which adds development support for the device.

## What We Know

- Apple's announcement says developers can [submit iPhone Duo optimized apps and games in App Store Connect](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=kkphp5qo) today.
- Per Apple, Xcode 27.1 includes Device Hub, which lets developers visualize how apps behave on iPhone Duo across all poses and orientations.
- [Apple Developer News](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=kkphp5qo) also describes a new preview tool in App Store Connect that shows how screenshots, product page headers and other metadata look on an iPhone Duo product page.
- [Apple's releases page](https://developer.apple.com/news/releases/) lists an Xcode 27.1 release candidate (build 27A9275) dated October 5, 2026, following the first Xcode 27.1 beta (build 27A9269) dated September 18, 2026.
- As [MacRumors reported](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/09/18/apple-releases-xcode-27-1-beta-iphone-duo-support/), that first beta ran only on Apple silicon Macs with macOS 26.6 or later and included refreshed SDKs for the iPhone Duo plus an updated simulator for the device's configurations and display orientations. The report places the release just over a week after Apple's "Surprise and Shine" event, where the iPhone Duo was introduced as Apple's first foldable smartphone.

## Why the SDK Matters

According to [MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/10/05/apple-opens-iphone-duo-app-submissions/), existing App Store apps will run on the iPhone Duo automatically. Only apps built with the iOS 27.1 SDK or later, however, can dynamically resize to fill the entire inner display; unoptimized apps will have black borders.

## Deadline Ahead

Apple states that starting April 2027, any apps or games submitted will need to include screenshots for iPhone Duo, per [Apple Developer News](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=kkphp5qo).

## What We Don't Know

The sources reviewed do not describe the contents of the Xcode 27.1 release notes beyond iPhone Duo support, and they do not say when the final, non-candidate Xcode 27.1 build will ship.
